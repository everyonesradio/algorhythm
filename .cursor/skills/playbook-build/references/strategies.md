# The 12 Product Strategies

Each strategy is a goal, a stack of plays that serve it, the metrics that prove it moved, and the
questions that reveal *why* it isn't moving. Plays appear in multiple stacks — that's intended.
The same play does different work depending on the goal it's serving.

Use this file two ways: **goal in** ("improve retention") → take the stack; or **metric in**
("Day 7 retention is 8%") → run the Behind the Data questions first, then pick the plays that
answer what you found.

## Onboarding
*Getting new users familiar with the product and achieving first value.*

**Play stack:** Empty States · Setup Defaults · Progressive Disclosure · Time to Value · Success Moments · JTBD Copywriting · Pattern Alignment · Sandbox Experience · Personalisation · Momentum Bias · Micro Interactions · Permission Serve · Loading Feedback · Gamified Progress · Contact Bridge

**Metrics**

| Metric | Formula | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Activation Rate | (Users who complete core action ÷ Total signups) × 100 | High activation means onboarding leads to meaningful engagement. Low activation signals friction, confusion, or a weak first impression |
| Time to Value (TTV) | Average time from signup to first core action | The faster the TTV, the less drop-off. Speed is retention's friend |
| Onboarding Completion Rate | (Users who finish all steps ÷ Users who started) × 100 | Completion signals the flow was clear, motivating, and relevant |
| Step-by-Step Drop-off Rate | (Users who dropped at step N ÷ Users who reached step N) × 100 | Reveals the exact point of friction where users abandon |
| Day 1/7/30 Retention | (Users active on day X ÷ Users who signed up X days ago) × 100 | Retention is the long tail of onboarding. Get Day 1 wrong and Day 30 doesn't matter |

**Behind the data**
- *Conversion funnel:* Which steps have the highest drop-off? What's the correlation between completion speed and retention? Do users who skip tutorials have lower activation rates?
- *Segmentation:* Which traffic sources produce the best-activating users? How does activation vary by device type? Do certain segments need different onboarding flows?
- *Behavioural patterns:* What's the optimal onboarding length? Do users convert after a certain event or usage threshold? How does time-of-day impact completion rates?

## Activation
*Getting users to experience core value and become active.*

**Play stack:** Time to Value · Success Moments · Setup Defaults · Empty States · Discovery · Personalisation · Micro Interactions · Momentum Bias · Progressive Disclosure · JTBD Copywriting · Loading Feedback · Sandbox Experience · Contact Bridge

**Metrics**

| Metric | Formula | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Activation Rate | (Users who complete core action ÷ Total signups) × 100 | Percentage of new users who reach first value |
| Time to Value (TTV) | Average time from signup to first core action | How quickly users experience product value |
| Feature Adoption Rate | (Users who used feature X ÷ Total activated users) × 100 | Higher adoption correlates with better retention |

**Behind the data**
- *Activation patterns:* What sequence of actions leads to fastest activation? Which features are the strongest activation predictors? How does guided vs self-directed exploration affect activation?
- *User journey:* Where do users get stuck before reaching activation? Which onboarding elements correlate with activation success? How does personalisation level impact activation depth?
- *Value discovery:* What triggers users' "aha" moments most effectively? Which empty states convert best to meaningful actions? How does social proof influence activation behaviour?

## Retention
*Keeping users engaged and coming back.*

**Play stack:** Gamified Progress · System Widget · Value Replay · Effort Moat · Personalisation · Variable Reward · Commitment · Success Moments · Micro Interactions · Intent Mirroring · Pattern Alignment · Deep-link

**Metrics**

| Metric | Formula | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1/7/30 Retention Rate | (Users active on Day X ÷ Install cohort size) × 100 | Foundation metric for sustainable growth |
| Stickiness Ratio (DAU/MAU) | Daily Active Users ÷ Monthly Active Users | Higher ratios indicate successful habit formation |
| Streak Length | Average consecutive days of app usage | Longer streaks predict long-term retention |

**Behind the data**
- *Retention curve:* What does your curve's shape indicate about product-market fit? Which cohorts have the flattest (best) curves? How do seasonal patterns affect long-term retention?
- *Feature usage evolution:* Which features predict long-term retention vs short-term engagement? How does feature adoption sequence impact retention strength? What's the optimal feature discovery pace?
- *Habit formation:* What triggers transform occasional users into habitual users? How does notification strategy impact retention without causing fatigue? Which workflows become most "sticky" over time?

## Growth & Viral
*Turning users into advocates and drivers of organic growth.*

**Play stack:** Referral · Shareability · Contact Bridge · Deep-link · Success Moments · Gamified Progress · Effort Moat · Variable Reward · System Widget · Spark Curiosity

**Metrics**

| Metric | Formula | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Viral Coefficient (K-Factor) | Invitations sent per user × Invitation acceptance rate | Indicates if your product grows itself through user sharing |
| Referral Program ROI | (Revenue from referred users − Referral costs) ÷ Referral costs × 100 | Whether your referral program is profitable |
| Organic Growth Rate | (New organic users ÷ Total users) × 100 per period | Tracks word-of-mouth and the strength of non-paid channels |
| Network Density Score | (Connected user pairs ÷ Total possible connections) × 100 | A denser network boosts retention, collaboration, and virality |
| Viral Cycle Time | Average time from signup to first successful referral | Faster cycles drive exponential growth |

**Behind the data**
- *Viral loop:* Which sharing moments generate highest-quality referrals? How does timing of referral prompts affect conversion? What's the optimal incentive structure for both referrer and referee?
- *Network effects:* At what network size do users experience significant value increases? Which collaborative features drive strongest viral behaviour? How does social proof influence referral willingness?
- *Channel performance:* Which referral channels have the highest LTV users? How do organic vs incentivised referrals differ in quality? What content types achieve the highest share-to-conversion rates?

## Monetisation
*Converting free users to paying customers.*

**Play stack:** The Paywall · Intentional Friction · Limited Offer · Perceived Effort Delay · Success Moments · Time to Value · Spark Curiosity · Gamified Progress · Effort Moat · Investment · Personalisation · JTBD Copywriting

**Metrics**

| Metric | Formula | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free-to-Paid Conversion Rate | (Paid subscribers ÷ Total free users) × 100 | Shows how well your product turns interest into income |
| Paywall Conversion Rate | (Users who upgraded ÷ Users who saw paywall) × 100 | Captures how persuasive your offer is at the decision moment |
| ARPU | Total revenue ÷ Total active users (free + paid) | Assesses overall monetisation health across your base |
| Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) | (ARPU × Gross margin %) ÷ Churn rate | Total revenue each customer brings before they churn |
| Trial-to-Paid Conversion Rate | (Trial users who converted ÷ Total trial users) × 100 | Reflects the strength of your product experience |
| Churn Rate | (Customers lost ÷ Total customers at start of period) × 100 | How quickly you're losing paying customers |

**Behind the data**
- *Conversion funnel:* At what point do users hit paywalls most frequently? Which premium features drive highest conversion intent? How does time-to-paywall affect conversion rates?
- *Pricing & packaging:* Which tiers have the highest conversion rates? How does annual vs monthly billing affect LTV? What's the optimal trial length for your product?
- *User behaviour:* Which actions predict highest conversion likelihood? How does usage frequency correlate with willingness to pay? What triggers cause users to cancel?

<a id="conversion-optimisation"></a>
## Conversion Optimisation
*Improving key conversion points throughout the funnel.*

**Play stack:** The Paywall · Limited Offer · Intentional Friction · Success Moments · Spark Curiosity · Momentum Bias · Time to Value · JTBD Copywriting · Perceived Effort Delay · Personalisation

**Metrics**

| Metric | Formula | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conversion Rate | (Users who completed target action ÷ Total users in funnel) × 100 | Core business metric that directly impacts revenue |
| Funnel Drop-off Rate | (Users who abandoned at step X ÷ Users who reached step X) × 100 | Identifies specific friction points to fix |
| Average Time to Convert | Average time from first touchpoint to conversion | Helps optimise nurturing and retargeting windows |
| Conversion Rate by Traffic Source | (Conversions per source ÷ Visits per source) × 100 | Shows where to invest budget for best ROI |

**Behind the data**
- *Funnel:* Which steps have the highest drop-off? How does mobile vs desktop performance compare? What's the optimal number of steps?
- *Traffic quality:* Which sources bring users most likely to convert? How do conversion rates vary by segment? What's the correlation between traffic quality and LTV?
- *Timing & urgency:* How does time spent in funnel correlate with conversion likelihood? Do limited-time offers meaningfully impact conversion? What's the optimal follow-up timing for abandoned conversions?

## Experience Refinement
*Adding delight and premium feel to interactions.*

**Play stack:** Micro Interactions · Small Quirk · Loading Feedback · Success Moments · Perceived Effort Delay · Pattern Alignment · JTBD Copywriting · Variable Reward · Progressive Disclosure

**Metrics**

| Metric | Formula | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task Success Rate | (Tasks completed successfully ÷ Total attempts) × 100 | Direct measure of usability and user frustration |
| Average Session Duration | Total time spent ÷ Number of sessions | Indicates whether users find the experience valuable enough to stay |
| User Satisfaction (CSAT/NPS) | Standard CSAT or NPS survey | Directly correlates with retention and word-of-mouth growth |
| Error Rate | (User errors or failed actions ÷ Total user actions) × 100 | High error rates indicate poor UX design or technical issues |

**Behind the data**
- *Usability:* Which tasks have the lowest success rates, and why? How does task performance vary across devices? What types of errors occur most frequently?
- *Experience quality:* Which interface elements contribute most to satisfaction? How do micro-interactions impact overall experience perception? What's the correlation between session duration and satisfaction?
- *Design effectiveness:* Which design patterns work best for your user base? How do loading states and feedback affect perceived performance? What level of customisation do users actually use?

## Trust Building
*Establishing credibility and user confidence.*

**Play stack:** Fail Safe · Pattern Alignment · JTBD Copywriting · Loading Feedback · Perceived Effort Delay · Micro Interactions · Success Moments · Permission Serve · Progressive Disclosure · Intentional Friction

**Metrics**

| Metric | Formula | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Permission Grant Rate | (Permissions granted ÷ Permission requests) × 100 | Direct indicator of user trust and comfort with your product |
| Task Completion Rate (complex flows) | (Multi-step tasks completed ÷ started) × 100 | Completion of advanced flows shows users trust the system enough to invest time |
| Support Ticket Volume (trust-related) | (Trust/security/privacy tickets ÷ Total tickets) × 100 | High volume suggests gaps in clarity or confidence in safety |

**Behind the data**
- *Permissions:* Which requests have the lowest grant rates? How does timing affect acceptance? Does providing clear explanations increase grant rates?
- *Flow confidence:* Where do users most commonly abandon complex tasks? Which flows benefit most from additional guidance or safety nets? How does task complexity correlate with completion?
- *Trust issues:* What types of concerns generate most support tickets? How quickly can trust-related issues be resolved? Which segments have the highest trust-related support needs?

## Habit Formation
*Creating sticky, repeated usage patterns.*

**Play stack:** Commitment · Gamified Progress · System Widget · Variable Reward · Value Replay · Investment · Personalisation · Success Moments · Micro Interactions · Effort Moat · Deep-link

**Metrics**

| Metric | Formula | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly Active Users (WAU) | Unique users active at least once per week | Regular weekly usage indicates habit formation better than daily spikes |
| Session Frequency | Average sessions per user per week | Frequent return visits are the core indicator of habit formation |
| Streak Completion Rate | (Users maintaining 7+ day streaks ÷ Users who start streaks) × 100 | Streaks are proven to reinforce habitual behaviour |
| Return Rate After 30+ Days | (Users who return after 30 days inactive ÷ Users inactive 30+ days) × 100 | Returning after long breaks signals meaningful product attachment |

**Behind the data**
- *Usage patterns:* What time of day do users most consistently engage? How does session frequency change over the first 30 days? Which users successfully transition from sporadic to regular usage?
- *Habit reinforcement:* How effective are different reminder strategies? What's the correlation between streak length and long-term retention? Which commitment mechanisms work best for different user types?
- *Behavioural triggers:* What external factors influence consistent usage? How do social features impact habit formation? Which progress indicators most effectively motivate continued use?

## Premium Positioning
*Making the product feel high-value and sophisticated.*

**Play stack:** Perceived Effort Delay · Small Quirk · Micro Interactions · Intentional Friction · Loading Feedback · Success Moments · JTBD Copywriting · Pattern Alignment · Progressive Disclosure

**Metrics**

| Metric | Formula | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ARPU | Total revenue ÷ Total number of users | Core indicator of premium pricing power and perceived value |
| Price Sensitivity | Conversion rate at different price points during testing | Shows if users perceive enough value to justify premium pricing |
| CSAT (premium features) | CSAT score specifically for premium/advanced features | Premium users should be more satisfied, to justify higher prices |

**Behind the data**
- *Premium value delivery:* Do premium users have higher satisfaction than basic users? Which premium features drive highest usage and satisfaction? How does premium retention compare to basic?
- *Pricing power:* How do conversion rates change at different price points? What's the optimal price increase that maintains conversion quality? Which segments show the lowest price sensitivity?
- *Premium experience:* Do premium touches (animations, delays, polish) correlate with higher ARPU? How does premium onboarding affect long-term value perception? Which positioning elements most effectively justify higher prices?
